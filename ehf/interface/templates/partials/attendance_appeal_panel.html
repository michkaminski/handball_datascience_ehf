Opponent Attendance Appeal Model (MAF)

A hierarchical Bayesian model separates the attendance strength of the host, the appeal of the opponent and the match conditions. An IAF index of 100 means a typical (average) opponent. Results come from the posterior distribution (MCMC), not from a single point estimate.

Scientific documentation of the model

The report filters above do not narrow the model: MAF is estimated once on all matches of the given gender (a separate training process). The model choice decides the gender; EHF models measure the number of spectators only (no venue capacity data).

Attendance simulator (predictive distribution)

The forecast combines parameter uncertainty (posterior samples) and the random variation of a future match (drawing the number of spectators). Predictive intervals are wider than the credible interval of the mean.

Capacity is optional: EHF models count spectators only. When given, it is used to show approximate fill rates and P(full house).

Deviations from expected attendance

Standardised residual z = (spectators − E[spectators]) / predictive SD ; p = two-sided predictive p-value (mid-p), i.e. the probability of an observation at least this extreme under the assumed model — without assuming normality.
